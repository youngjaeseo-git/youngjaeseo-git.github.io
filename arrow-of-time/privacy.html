Arrow of Time Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

1. Information the developer collects directly

Arrow of Time (the "app") has no account signup, and the developer does not send personal information to a server the developer operates.

Game progress (cleared levels, settings, a board in progress, and whether ads were removed) is stored only on the player's device and is deleted when the app is deleted.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows Google AdMob ads. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process the advertising identifier, device and operating-system information, IP address, and ad interaction data.

The controller and the method follow the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and Google's information for advertising technology partners (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

You can reset the advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in the device settings. Where the law requires consent, a consent screen is shown when the app starts, and you can change that choice later under Settings > Ad privacy choices.

3. In-app purchases

"Remove ads" payments are processed by Google Play or the App Store. The developer does not receive or store the player's payment-method details. Only the purchase record provided by the store is used to confirm whether the item was bought.

4. Children's information

The app does not ask for age, and the developer does not collect personal information from children. Processing that happens while ads are served follows section 2.

5. Sharing with third parties and transfers abroad

The developer does not provide personal information to third parties. The processing in sections 2 and 3 happens on Google's and Apple's systems, which may use servers outside the player's country.

6. Your rights

Game data stored on the device can be erased at any time by deleting the app or by clearing the app's data in the device settings. Rights related to advertising follow the Google information in section 2.

7. Contact

contact@grassfieldstudio.com

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the update will be announced through an app update and the store page.
